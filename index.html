Method

The benchmark evaluates dubbing quality only. Video resolution and bitrate were explicitly excluded from scoring.

Inputs

  • Original source clip plus five anonymous dubbed candidates.
  • Only the first 60 seconds of each video were evaluated.
  • Candidate order was shuffled in every round.
  • Provider names and filenames were not included in prompts.

Criteria

  • Translation accuracy and conversational intent.
  • Spoken naturalness in the target language.
  • Voice similarity, speaker separation, and timing alignment.
  • Primary metric: top-1 votes across 10 rounds.

Source videos

No media files are redistributed in this repository. Source links and clip ranges are listed for reproducibility.
